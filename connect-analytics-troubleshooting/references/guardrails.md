# Amazon Connect Contact Lens & Wisdom Anti-Misdiagnosis Guardrails

Read these guardrails BEFORE concluding on any Contact Lens or Wisdom issue.

## Guardrail 1: Contact Lens Requires Instance AND Contact Flow Enablement
Contact Lens must be enabled at the instance level AND configured in the contact flow using the "Set recording and analytics behavior" block. Enabling only one produces no analytics. Both are required for transcription, sentiment, and categories.

## Guardrail 2: Real-Time vs Post-Contact Analytics Are Different
Real-time analytics (transcript, sentiment, categories) are available only during active calls via the real-time API. Post-contact analytics are generated after the call ends and stored in S3. They use different APIs and have different availability windows.

## Guardrail 3: Contact Lens and Wisdom Are Separate Features
Contact Lens provides analytics (transcription, sentiment, categories). Wisdom provides agent assist (knowledge base search, recommendations). They are separate features with separate enablement, pricing, and APIs. Do not conflate them.

## Guardrail 4: Sentiment Scores Have a Defined Range
Sentiment scores range from -5 (most negative) to +5 (most positive). Scores are provided per conversation turn and as an overall score. A score of 0 is neutral. Do not invent different scoring ranges or interpret scores outside this range.

## Guardrail 5: Transcription Accuracy Is Not 100%
Transcription accuracy depends on audio quality, language, accents, background noise, and domain-specific terminology. Custom vocabulary improves accuracy for specific terms but does not guarantee perfect transcription. Always note transcription limitations.

## Guardrail 6: Category Rules Require Published Status
Category rules must be in PUBLISHED status to be evaluated. Draft rules are not applied to contacts. Rules can be configured for real-time or post-contact evaluation. Real-time rules have processing latency.

## Guardrail 7: Wisdom Knowledge Base Requires Indexing
Content uploaded to a Wisdom knowledge base must be indexed before it is searchable. Indexing is asynchronous and can take minutes to hours depending on content volume. New content is not immediately available for search.

## Guardrail 8: Screen Recording Is Separate from Call Recording
Screen recording captures the agent's screen during a contact. Call recording captures audio. They are separate features with separate storage, configuration, and retention settings. Do not confuse screen recording with call recording.

## Guardrail 9: Data Lake Export Has Processing Delay
Contact Lens data exported to the data lake (S3) has a processing delay. Data is not available immediately after the contact ends. Typical delay is 15-60 minutes. Do not assume missing data means a configuration error if the contact recently ended.

## Guardrail 10: Custom Vocabulary Has Size and Format Limits
Custom vocabulary files have size limits and must follow a specific format (phrase, IPA, SoundsLike, DisplayAs). Invalid entries are silently skipped. Large vocabularies can increase processing time. Verify vocabulary entries are correctly formatted.

## Guardrail 11: Evaluation Forms Require Agent and Contact Association
Evaluation forms are filled out for specific contacts by evaluators. They are not automatically generated by Contact Lens. Evaluation forms use Contact Lens data (transcript, sentiment) as input but require human or automated evaluation submission.

## Guardrail 12: S3 Bucket Permissions Must Include Connect Service
The S3 bucket used for Contact Lens output, call recordings, and screen recordings must grant write permissions to the Amazon Connect service. Bucket policies must allow the connect.amazonaws.com service principal. Missing permissions cause silent data loss.
